question: "Do I need an account to order?"
order: 16
---

No, you can check out as a guest with just your email and delivery address.

An account does make life easier, though: it saves your addresses, shows all your orders in one place, lets you buy again in one click and collects loyalty points on every order. You can create one after checkout too.
