question: "How much does shipping cost?"
order: 0

Standard shipping is 4.90 EUR plus a small amount per kilogram, and it's free on orders over 49.00 EUR. Express costs 9.90 EUR plus a per-kilogram amount.

The exact price for your country appears at checkout before you pay, so there are no surprises.
