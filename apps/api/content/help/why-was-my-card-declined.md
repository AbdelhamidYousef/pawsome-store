question: "Why was my card declined?"
order: 13
---

A card is usually declined by the bank, not by us. The most common reasons are a typo in the card number, expiry date or security code, not enough funds, or a security check that wasn't completed.

Check the details and try again. If it still doesn't work, try another card or ask your bank. Nothing is charged for a declined payment.
