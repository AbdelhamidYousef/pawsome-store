question: "My parcel is late, what should I do?"
order: 5
---

First, check the track order page: it shows the latest status and where your parcel is.

If the delivery window has passed and the parcel still hasn't arrived, contact us with your order number. We'll chase it with the carrier and, if it's lost, send a replacement or a refund.
