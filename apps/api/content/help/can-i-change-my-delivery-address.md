question: "Can I change my delivery address after ordering?"
order: 3
---

Yes, as long as we haven't started packing. Contact us as soon as you can with your order number and the new address, and we'll update it.

Once an order is being packed, the label is already printed and we can no longer change it. If that happens, the tracking page shows the carrier, and most carriers let you redirect a parcel from their own site.
