## Who we are

Pawsome is a small team of pet people who got tired of toys that fell apart by Tuesday and beds that couldn't survive a wash. So we started making and choosing the things we wanted for our own dogs, cats, birds, fish, reptiles and small pets, and we opened a shop for everyone else.

## What we care about

- **Things that last.** Toys and treats are chosen for how they survive real chewing, zooming and pouncing, not how they look on a shelf.
- **Honest ingredients and safe materials.** Short ingredient lists, washable fabrics and nothing we wouldn't let our own pets near.
- **Tested at home.** Every product goes through our household panel of very fussy pets before it reaches the shop.
- **Making it right.** If your pet isn't impressed, tell us. We'll help you swap, return or find something better.

## Our labels

Alongside our own Pawsome range, we work with five specialist labels: Burrow & Biscuit for cosy beds and small-batch bakes, Moonbeam Mews for cats, Scaleby for reptile habitats, Pebble & Pine for natural materials and Tidepool for aquariums.

## Giving back

Every round-up at checkout and every loyalty point you donate goes towards Whisker Hollow Rescue, the shelter we support.
