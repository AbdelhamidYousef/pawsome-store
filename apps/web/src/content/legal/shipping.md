## Delivery options

- **Standard:** 4.90 EUR plus a per-kg amount, 3 to 5 working days.
- **Express:** 9.90 EUR plus a per-kg amount, 1 to 2 working days.

Standard shipping is free for orders over 49.00 EUR.

## Dispatch times

Orders placed before 15:00 (Amsterdam time) on a working day are packed and dispatched the same day. Orders placed later, at weekends or on public holidays go out on the next working day.

## Where we deliver

The checkout lists the countries we deliver to. Choose your country in the address step to see the options and prices for it.

## Tracking

Once your order is packed you can follow it on the track order page with your order number and email. We also email you when it ships.

## Something not right?

If your parcel is late or arrives damaged, the help centre explains what to do next, or you can contact us directly.
