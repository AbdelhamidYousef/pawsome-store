question: "How do I withdraw from my order?"
order: 8
---

Use the withdrawal form linked in the footer. Enter your order number and email, choose the items (or the whole order), and send it. We confirm by email with the date and time we received it.

In the EU you can withdraw within 14 days of receiving your order, without giving a reason. The returns page explains how to send the items back.
