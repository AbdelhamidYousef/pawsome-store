We use your details only to take and deliver your orders, send order emails, handle returns and answer your messages. We never sell your data and we don't run advertising or tracking scripts.

The privacy notice explains exactly what we collect, who helps us process it and how long we keep it. You can ask for a copy of your data, or have it deleted, through the contact page.
