question: "How do I change my details?"
order: 18
---

Sign in and open My account. The profile section lets you change your name, email and password, and the addresses section lets you add, edit or remove saved addresses and choose a default.

Changes apply to future orders. To change the address on an order you've already placed, see "Can I change my delivery address?".
