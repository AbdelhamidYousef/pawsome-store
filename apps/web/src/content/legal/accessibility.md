## Our aim

We want everyone to be able to shop at Pawsome, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they use. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we do

- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator.
- Forms have visible labels and clear, specific error messages.
- Colours are chosen for contrast, and nothing relies on colour alone.
- Images that carry meaning have text descriptions.
- Animations respect your system's reduced-motion setting.
- We check new pages with automated accessibility tests before they go live.

## Known gaps

We have not yet had a full external audit. Some customer-uploaded photos may have short or generic descriptions.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something on the site is hard to use, tell us through the contact page. Let us know the page and what happened, and we will reply within five working days.
