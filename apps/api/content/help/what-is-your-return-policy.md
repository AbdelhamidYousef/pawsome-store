question: "What is your return policy?"
order: 6
---

You can return unused items within 30 days of delivery. Food, treats and anything hygiene-sensitive must be sealed and unopened.

If you're in the EU, the legal 14-day right of withdrawal applies as well. Faulty or damaged items are always on us. The returns page has the full details.
