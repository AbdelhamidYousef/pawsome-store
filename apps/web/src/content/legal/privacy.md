## The short version

We collect only what we need to take and deliver your order, look after your account and answer your messages. We never sell your data, and we do not run advertising or tracking scripts.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email and a password, which we store only as a salted hash.
- **Order details:** the items you buy, your delivery address and the email you used.
- **Pet profiles:** if you add a pet, its name, type and the details you choose to share, so the shop can suggest the right sizes and food.
- **Messages:** anything you send us through the contact, withdrawal or returns forms.

## How we use it

To process and deliver orders, send order and delivery emails, handle returns and refunds, and reply to you. If you sign up for our newsletter, we also send it to you; every issue has an unsubscribe link.

## Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the shop: our payment provider (Stripe), who handles card details so they never touch our servers, and the email service that sends your order updates.

## How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as accounting rules require. Account and pet details stay until you delete your account or ask us to.

## Your choices

You can see and change most of your details in your account. To get a copy of your data or have it deleted, contact us through the contact page and we will reply within 30 days.
