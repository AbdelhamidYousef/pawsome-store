question: "How do I return an item?"
order: 7
---

Open the order in your account and choose Return items, or contact us with your order number and the items you'd like to send back.

We'll reply with a return reference and the address to post it to. Pack the items in their original packaging if you can, and write the reference on the parcel. Your refund is on its way as soon as the items arrive.
