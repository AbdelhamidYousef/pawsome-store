## The short version

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the shop to work. We set no analytics and no marketing cookies.

## The cookies we set

- **Session:** keeps you signed in. Lasts until you sign out or it expires.
- **Cart:** remembers what is in your basket. Lasts 30 days.
- **Referral:** only if you arrive through a friend's "refer a friend" link: remembers the friend's code for 30 days so it can be attached when you create an account. It holds that code and nothing else, and is removed once you register.

## Why we do not ask for consent

Under EU rules, cookies that are strictly necessary to provide something you asked for (like a cart) do not need consent. If we ever add analytics or marketing, we will ask first and nothing will load before you choose.

## Your notice choice

The notice you saw is remembered in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
