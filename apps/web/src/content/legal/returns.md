## Returns in 30 days

Changed your mind, or your pet simply isn't a fan? You can send back unused items within 30 days of delivery. Food, treats and anything hygiene-sensitive must be sealed and unopened.

## How it works

1. Start a return from your order in your account, or contact us with your order number and the items you want to send back.
2. We reply with the return steps and a reference to write on the parcel.
3. When the items arrive, we check them and refund you to the original payment method, usually within five working days.

## Damaged or faulty items

If something arrives damaged or stops working, tell us within 14 days and include a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, and you won't pay to send it back.

## Your right to withdraw

If you are in the EU you may withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason. Use the withdrawal form linked in the footer. We confirm it by email with the date and time we received it.
